How {{ s.name }} works

{{ s.name }} reads {{ channels.values()|map(attribute='sources')|map('length')|sum }} sources across {{ channels|length }} channels every {{ s.schedule('collect_every_minutes', 30) }} minutes and keeps only what is worth your time.

  1. Collect. RSS, JSON feeds and job APIs. Duplicate links and syndicated copies are dropped. Old articles are archived and never pushed as "today".
  2. Gatekeep. A quick check removes off-topic items, spam and pay-to-apply schemes.
  3. Score twice. The AI rates every item twice, independently. Both scores must clear a bar that is lower for official sources and higher for aggregators.
  4. Write. A clear headline, an answer-first summary and "why it matters". For opportunities it also extracts deadline, funding, eligibility and whether Pakistanis can apply.
  5. Group. Reports about the same event are grouped, so you see one story with all its sources.
  6. Rank by heat. Stories covered by more independent sources rank higher. Heat fades over time.
  7. Brief. Every morning a ranked briefing per channel, plus a weekly roundup.

Everything links to the original source. The AI runs locally with Ollama. When no model is running, transparent keyword rules take over.
